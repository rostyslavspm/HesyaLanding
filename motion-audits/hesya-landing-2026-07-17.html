02

Where the timings land

Scroll-bound motion can sit in the deliberate band. Nav, menus, and quote swaps should live left of 300ms. Infinite loops sit outside the scale entirely.

The animations

  2. 2FAQ accordion200ms

  4. 4CTA magnetic lift~250ms
  5. 5Tab underline stroke550ms

  9. 9Lenis / pin scroll1200ms+
Instant / Responsive · 0–300ms — menus, FAQ, dropdowns, taps.
Deliberate · 300–500ms — earned by large surfaces and brand moments.
Sluggish · 500ms+ — reserve for rare cinematic scroll; not for quote swaps.
What's off

The highest-frequency chrome gap (mobile menu) has zero duration. The quote rotator sits at 700ms for a content swap that fires every 10s. Manifesto reveal stretches toward 1s while ambient loops never stop.

CriticalJakub

Mobile menu mounts and unmounts with no enter or exit

WhatMobileMenu returns null when closed. Open/close is a hard tree mount — overlay and sheet appear and vanish instantly. No opacity, no slide, no exit hold.

Why it mattersThis is the textbook half-built motion gap. A full-bleed overlay that pops breaks the calm polish of everything else on the page. Users notice absence of motion more than subtle motion.

Recommended motionKeep the sheet mounted through exit: dim backdrop 200ms, panel slides from the right with opacity (300ms, --ease-hesya / ease-out-quint). Exit mirrors enter, slightly faster (240ms). CSS transitions or a short GSAP timeline — no Framer required.

Desktop Features dropdown is opacity-only, not origin-aware

WhatThe nav menu toggles opacity + visibility over 200ms. Timing is fine; shape is not — it fades from nowhere instead of expanding from the trigger.

Why it mattersOrigin-aware motion ties the panel to its button. Opacity alone reads as a floating layer, not a menu.

Recommended motionKeep 180–220ms. Add transform-origin: top left, translateY(-6px) scale(0.98) → identity with opacity. Exit: reverse, slightly faster.

Feature panels keep will-change on permanently

What.feature-content-panel and .feature-phone-screen set will-change: transform, opacity (and opacity) in CSS, always — not only while pinned/scrubbing.

Why it mattersPermanent promotion burns compositor memory for layers that are idle most of the visit. Jakub’s production rule: target will-change only when motion is imminent.

Recommended motionAdd a class (e.g. .is-animating) while the pin trigger is active, or set/clear will-change in the ScrollTrigger onToggle callback. Idle state: will-change: auto.

CriticalEmilAnti-checklist

Three infinite ambient loops run on the first viewport

WhatambientBreath (24s) on every .glass-panel, hero-phone-float (10s) on the phone, and hero-float (8s) on flying cards. All infinite; none pauseable. Anti-checklist: pulsing / breathe loops on UI surfaces.

Why it mattersA brand selling calm should not keep three layers gently thrashing forever. Individually subtle; stacked, they read as “always animating.” Reduced-motion users are fine — everyone else pays a continuous tax.

Recommended motionKeep at most one ambient layer (prefer glass at 30s+, <1% scale). Kill phone float or flying-card float — the pointer drift already supplies life. No pause UI needed if loops are gone.
